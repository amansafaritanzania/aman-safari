The Kilimanjaro Experience

A mountain journey, not just a summit.

Kilimanjaro rises above northern Tanzania and offers a unique trekking experience through changing landscapes toward the summit.

A successful climb is about preparation, sensible pacing, route choice, acclimatization and a strong support team. Your itinerary can also include safari or other Tanzania experiences before or after the trek.

Prepare With Purpose

Good preparation makes the journey better.

Before booking, consider your fitness, trekking experience, travel dates, route length and acclimatization needs. A qualified local mountain team should guide the operational and safety decisions.

The Northern Circuit experience

A classic safari route, thoughtfully paced.

This six-day route is designed for travellers who want to experience three of northern Tanzania's best-known wildlife areas without trying to fit the entire circuit into a few days.

Begin in Arusha, explore Tarangire, continue into the Serengeti, then spend time around the Ngorongoro Conservation Area before returning to Arusha.

We can adapt the route to your travel dates, group size, preferred accommodation and interests. Wildlife sightings and seasonal animal movements are natural and cannot be guaranteed.

What makes this journey special

Each destination brings a different landscape and safari experience. The final order may be adjusted to suit lodge locations, road conditions and travel dates.

Stop 01

Tarangire

Known for its baobab-studded scenery and large elephant herds, Tarangire offers a memorable introduction to the northern safari circuit.

Stop 02

Serengeti

Wide-open grasslands, varied habitats and rich wildlife viewing make the Serengeti a centrepiece of this itinerary.

Stop 03

Ngorongoro

Explore the crater area, a remarkable highland landscape with a distinctive concentration of wildlife on its crater floor.

Your journey, day by day

The proposed 6-day itinerary

This is the starting plan for your quote. Exact departure times, lodge names and the final route are confirmed after checking your dates and supplier availability.

Day 01

Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Meet your safari team in Arusha and travel to Tarangire for an afternoon game drive. Enjoy the park's distinctive scenery and look for elephants and other wildlife before heading to your overnight stay.

Overnight: Tarangire / Manyara area
Day 02

Tarangire to Central Serengeti

Continue toward the Serengeti through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. This is a longer transfer day, with wildlife viewing along the route where conditions and timing allow.

Overnight: Central Serengeti area
Day 03

A full day in the Serengeti

Spend the day exploring the Serengeti with your guide. The route and game-drive areas can be adjusted according to the season, recent wildlife information and your interests.

Overnight: Central Serengeti area
Day 04

Serengeti to the Karatu highlands

Enjoy a morning game drive before travelling toward the Ngorongoro highlands and Karatu. Your overnight location will be selected to make the following day's crater visit practical.

Overnight: Karatu area
Day 05

Ngorongoro Crater experience

Set out for a crater visit, subject to the applicable conservation rules, permits and crater-service arrangements. Enjoy a guided wildlife-viewing experience before returning to the Karatu area.

Overnight: Karatu area
Day 06

Return to Arusha

After breakfast, travel back to Arusha. If you have a flight or another onward connection, share the timing with us so the return plan can be checked before confirmation.

Safari ends in Arusha
Planning note: Day 2 is a substantial transfer and the exact overnight locations affect driving time. The route should be reviewed with the operating guide and accommodation suppliers before it is sold as a confirmed departure.

What will be clearly stated in your quote?

Before you decide, the written proposal should identify exactly what is included and what is not included.

  • Safari vehicle, guide and transport arrangements.
  • Accommodation properties, room basis and meal plan.
  • Applicable TANAPA and NCAA charges, shown separately where practical.
  • Crater-service, vehicle or other authority charges where applicable.
  • Transfers, drinking water and any agreed extras.

Commonly excluded unless specifically listed: international flights, visas, travel insurance, tips, personal purchases and optional activities.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a confirmed departure?

No. This is a sample itinerary. The operating team must confirm the route, accommodation, vehicle, guide and availability before any booking is accepted.

Can the itinerary be changed?

Yes. The number of nights, accommodation level and travel pace can be discussed. Changes may affect the final cost and route.

Do all travellers pay the same park fees?

Not necessarily. The relevant authority's visitor category and required supporting documents can affect the applicable charges. These will be checked for your group.

Will we definitely see the Big Five or the Great Migration?

Wildlife is wild and seasonal. Your guide can plan around current conditions, but no animal sighting or migration location can be guaranteed.

How do I reserve?

Start by sending an enquiry. The responsible safari operator will review the details and provide a written proposal with current costs and next steps. A booking is only confirmed through the operator's formal confirmation process.

The Tarangire Experience

A different rhythm of safari.

Tarangire is known for its striking baobab landscapes, elephants and wildlife-rich areas that make it a memorable part of a northern Tanzania itinerary.

Its character changes with the seasons, making travel timing an important part of planning. Tarangire can also work beautifully alongside destinations such as Ngorongoro and Serengeti.

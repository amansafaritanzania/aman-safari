The Ruaha Experience

Space, wildlife and a wilder feeling.

Ruaha offers a distinctive safari atmosphere, with expansive landscapes and a reputation for memorable wildlife viewing away from the busiest northern circuit.

It can stand alone as a southern Tanzania journey or be combined with other destinations when your schedule and route allow. The best itinerary depends on the season, interests and travel style of your group.

Aman Safari Tanzania · Payment information

Clear steps. Verified payment instructions.

Payment comes after your itinerary and price have been agreed. Use the instructions on your written invoice, and contact us to verify anything that does not match.

Before making a payment

Know what you're paying for.

We first confirm your travel dates, itinerary, inclusions and total amount. Your payment instructions should match that written confirmation.

Important payment safety checkFor your protection, personal account numbers and recipient details are not published on this page. Request the current instructions after receiving your written itinerary, then verify the recipient name, amount and reference with Aman Safari through the official contact details below. Never share a PIN, password, one-time code or banking login.

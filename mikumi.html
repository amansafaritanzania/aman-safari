The Destination

A Classic Safari Within Easy Reach

Mikumi National Park is shaped by the open Mkata floodplain, woodland and seasonal water sources. Its accessible wildlife-viewing areas make it a strong choice for a shorter southern Tanzania safari.

MkataFloodplain
3,230 km²Park Area
SouthernSafari Circuit

Elephants, lions, buffalo, giraffes, zebras, hippos and many other species can be encountered here. The park also supports rich birdlife and guided activities that add variety beyond the classic game drive.

Signature Experiences

Explore the Floodplain & Beyond

Game Drives

Follow the tracks around the Mkata floodplain and open savannah in search of elephants, lions, buffalo and other wildlife.

Guided Walks

Experience the bush at a slower pace, with attention to tracks, plants, birds and the smaller details of the ecosystem.

Birding

Acacia woodland and seasonal pans attract rollers, bee-eaters, hornbills, raptors and other birdlife.

Why Include Mikumi?

A Smart Choice for the Southern Circuit

Wildlife Access

Open plains and a good network of tracks can make wildlife viewing rewarding without requiring a long expedition.

Dar Connection

Mikumi is accessible by road from Dar es Salaam and can work well for travelers with limited time.

Easy Pairing

Build a broader southern journey by combining Mikumi with Nyerere, Ruaha or other nearby experiences.

Best Time By Month

Choose Your Travel Month

Dry months generally make wildlife easier to spot around water, while greener months bring lush scenery and strong birding conditions.

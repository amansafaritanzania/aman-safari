The Lake Manyara Experience

A small park with a rich mix of landscapes.

Lake Manyara National Park brings together lake, woodland and open habitats in a compact setting that can add variety to a northern Tanzania itinerary.

It is often useful as part of a wider route through northern Tanzania, especially when combined with Tarangire, Ngorongoro or Serengeti.

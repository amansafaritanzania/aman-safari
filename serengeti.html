Choose your month with purpose.

Jan–Mar Green landscapes & seasonal wildlife activity
Apr–May Greener scenery & generally quieter travel periods
Jun–Jul Drier conditions & seasonal planning
Aug–Sep Popular period for wildlife-focused trips

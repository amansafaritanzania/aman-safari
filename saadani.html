The Destination

Safari Where Coast Meets Savanna

Saadani National Park brings together coastal savanna, mangroves, the Wami River and Indian Ocean beaches. It is Tanzania’s signature bush-and-beach safari setting.

1,100+ km²Protected Landscape
Wami RiverBoat Safari
Indian OceanCoastal Experience

Saadani is different from a classic inland safari. Here, wildlife habitat runs into mangroves, river channels and the Indian Ocean, creating a relaxed coastal rhythm alongside game viewing.

Signature Experiences

Wildlife, River & Ocean

Game Drives

Explore palm-studded savanna and coastal thickets for elephants, giraffe, buffalo, zebra and other wildlife. Sightings are never guaranteed.

Wami River Boat Safari

Cruise the Wami River with a guide and look for hippos, crocodiles, waterbirds and mangrove-lined channels.

Beach & Turtle Season

Explore the Indian Ocean coastline and, during the appropriate season, learn about green turtle nesting while following conservation guidance.

Why Include Saadani?

A Rare Bush-to-Beach Journey

Coast & Wildlife

Saadani lets travelers combine wildlife viewing with coastal scenery without building a completely separate beach holiday.

River Safari

The Wami River adds a different perspective, with boat-based wildlife and bird viewing through mangroves and river channels.

Beach Extension

The combination of mountain forest and Beach & Turtle Season gives the destination a character unlike the northern and southern circuits.

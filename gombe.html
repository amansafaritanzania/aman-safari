The Destination

Chimpanzee Country on Lake Tanganyika

Gombe National Park is a narrow strip of forest along the eastern shore of Lake Tanganyika, best known for its chimpanzees and immersive forest-walking experience.

ChimpanzeesSignature Wildlife
TanganyikaLake Shore
ForestWalking Safari

Gombe is different from Tanzania's open-savana destinations. The experience centers on guided walks through forest habitat, where visitors may encounter chimpanzees, red-tailed monkeys, baboons and rich birdlife.

Signature Experiences

Slow Down. Look Closer.

Chimpanzee Tracking

Walk with trained guides through forest trails in search of Gombe's chimpanzee communities. Sightings are never guaranteed.

Forest Walks

Explore steep forest paths while learning about tracks, plants, birds and the living ecosystem around you.

Lake Tanganyika

Pair the forest experience with the dramatic shoreline, clear water and peaceful scenery of one of Africa's great lakes.

Why Include Gombe?

A Completely Different Tanzania

Primate Focus

For travelers fascinated by chimpanzees and primates, Gombe offers a much more focused wildlife experience than a traditional game-drive safari.

Intimate Wilderness

Forest trails and limited access create a quieter, more personal rhythm than Tanzania's large open parks.

Lake & Forest

The combination of mountain forest and Lake Tanganyika gives the destination a character unlike the northern and southern circuits.

The Destination

A Different Rhythm of Safari

Nyerere brings together river landscapes, lakes, miombo woodland and open country. Its Rufiji River system creates opportunities to experience Tanzania from both land and water.

RufijiRiver System
Wild DogsPredator Focus
SouthernSafari Circuit

The park is known for boat safaris, game drives and guided walking experiences. Wildlife includes elephants, hippos, crocodiles, lions, buffalo, giraffes and African wild dogs. Sightings are never guaranteed, but the variety of habitats creates a distinctive safari experience.

Signature Experiences

See Nyerere From More Than One Angle

Rufiji Boat Safari

Glide along river channels and lakes while watching for hippos, crocodiles, elephants and birdlife around the water.

Wildlife Drives

Explore floodplains and woodland in search of elephants, lions, buffalo, giraffes and other southern-circuit wildlife.

Guided Walks

Experience the landscape at a slower pace with authorized guides, reading tracks, plants and the smaller signs of the bush.

Why Include Nyerere?

For Travelers Who Want Wilderness With Variety

Water & Wildlife

The Rufiji and its connected waterways add a dimension that a vehicle-only safari cannot provide.

Wild Dog Country

Nyerere is an important stronghold for African wild dogs alongside other predators and plains wildlife.

Southern Circuit

A strong choice for a quieter southern itinerary and a natural pairing with Ruaha or other southern destinations.
